# Pour Decisions

A bet list app for drink nights. Everyone picks their games, you take turns spinning the wheel to see what gets played next, and the app keeps score of who won and who drank.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No install or build step. To host it, turn on GitHub Pages for this repo and visit `/pour-decisions/`.

## How a night works

1. **Lineup**: add players and have each one pick 10 games (the number is adjustable). Shuffle the turn order if you like.
2. **Spin**: the player whose turn it is spins the wheel. Choose whether the wheel holds everyone's remaining games or only the spinner's.
3. **Record**: pick the winner (or tie), set the shots on the line (¼, ½, ¾, 1, 1½, 2, 3, or any quarter amount), and fine-tune who drinks and how much. Hit **Pour it** and the shot glass fills up.
4. **Shots**: the shot board shows each player's glass, total shots, and wins. Penalty buttons add ¼, ½, or 1 shot at any time.
5. **History**: wrap up the night to save it. The hall of fame tracks wins and shots across every night, matched by player name.

## Chaos roll

The **Chaos roll** button in the header opens a random number picker you can use any time for alterations, tiebreaks, or punishments. Set a min and max and roll it as a wheel, a coin, or a die.

## Themes

Xbox, PlayStation, Metal Gear, Card night, Nintendo, and Castlevania. Each one changes the colors, fonts, wheel, liquid in the shot glass, and the lines the app says to you.

## Saving

Everything is saved automatically in the browser you use (localStorage). Use **History → Backup** to copy or download a backup and restore it on another device.
